MacBook Air
Liquid Cooling

Designed and modified a 2017 MacBook Air, replacing its stock fan-based cooling system with a custom liquid-cooling solution.

PROJECT TYPE Personal Project
CATEGORY Hardware Modification
FOCUS Thermal Management
OVERVIEW

Rethinking the cooling system.

This project explored the possibility of replacing the stock cooling solution of a 2017 MacBook Air with a custom liquid-cooling system.

Rather than simply using the original fan-based thermal management system, I investigated how a liquid-cooling solution could be integrated into the compact architecture of the laptop.

DESIGN

Designing around constraints.

One of the biggest challenges was the extremely limited internal space of the MacBook Air.

Every component of the cooling system had to be considered in relation to the existing motherboard, chassis, battery, and other internal components.

The design process focused on finding a configuration that could provide effective thermal transfer while remaining compatible with the physical constraints of the laptop.

IMPLEMENTATION

Turning the design into hardware.

After developing the cooling concept, the system was physically integrated into the MacBook Air.

The modification required careful handling of the laptop's internal components and precise placement of the cooling hardware.

This stage of the project provided hands-on experience with hardware modification, mechanical constraints, and thermal management.

RESULTS

What I learned.

This project gave me a practical understanding of the challenges involved in designing thermal systems for compact electronics.

More importantly, it reinforced the importance of designing around real-world constraints. A technically effective solution still needs to physically fit, interact safely with existing components, and remain practical to implement.
